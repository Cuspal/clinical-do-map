# Example research priorities from the original procedure-mapping request

Target: active standard SNOMED concepts in the OMOP Procedure domain, using the supplied local vocabulary snapshot.

Review the entire catalogue across all score ranges. A score above 0.85 is useful for triage, but is not sufficient approval evidence. Correct automatic targets when the replacement is well supported. Broader targets may be accepted when documented and adequate for the intended use; otherwise flag them.

Research questions:

1. STEMI: characteristics and outcomes.
2. Complications as factors associated with mortality.
3. Peri-procedural complication rates.
4. Surgical site infection (SSI).
5. Mortality phenotype: who dies and why.
6. 30-day readmission.
7. Primary PCI within 90 minutes versus outcomes.
8. Stroke thrombolysis within 270 minutes versus outcomes.

Preserve distinctions between angiography and intervention, initial implantation and device management/removal, performance and preparation/standby, and treatment versus report/billing categories. Do not infer outcomes or timing thresholds from a procedure code. Define the study's time origins and link actual event timestamps separately. Navigation tags do not establish these phenotypes.
